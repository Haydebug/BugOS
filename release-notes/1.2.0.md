The Quick Access update.

**Quick Access (new)**
- Swipe two fingers in from the right edge of the trackpad: your shortcuts slide in on the right of whichever screen you're on. Slide down to pick, lift to open, swipe back out to close.
- Its own list, separate from the quick corner's. By default: Recent, Music, Browser, Squiggle, Search, All apps, Activity Monitor, Mission Control, File Explorer.
- **Recent: <app>** sits on top, so a quick swipe in and release flips between your last two apps.
- Optional keyboard shortcut (none until you set one): ↑ ↓ to pick, Return to open, Esc to close.
- Notification Center's swipe is turned off while Quick Access is on (it still opens from the clock), and comes back if you turn Quick Access off or uninstall.

**Quick Corner**
- New default fan: File Explorer, Mission Control, Squiggle, Music, Browser, Activity Monitor, All apps, Search, BugOS Settings, System Settings.
- New shortcuts: Music (Spotify or Apple Music), Browser (your default or pick one), AI (Claude, Codex or ChatGPT, shown by name), Discord, Recent.
- Add any app, file or folder with +.
- Curve setting (straight to very curved), tile colour and icon colour.
- The Squiggle shortcut now starts a snip.

**Taskbar**
- Hover Spotify or Music for previous, play/pause and next under the preview, like Windows 11.
- Hides itself for full-screen apps and in Mission Control.
- All apps is centred on the screen in Liquid Glass, with sorting by name, recently used, date added or size.
- No more menu-bar icons for the taskbar or Squiggle: everything is on the BugOS page.

**File Explorer**
- Applications in the navigation pane; choose and reorder which sections show.
- Files show the icon of the app that opens them, like Windows (or Mac style).
- The app icon can be light or dark on its own, separate from the window's appearance.

**Squiggle**
- Snips freeze the screen the instant you press the key, so open menus and hover states are in the shot.
- A proper app icon.

**Everything else**
- New black BugOS icon.
- Fixes: installing sometimes left BugOS closed; Quick Access drawing blank; Discord's icon.
