Handoff is back.

**Handoff on the taskbar**
- When your iPhone or iPad offers the app you're using (Handoff), its icon now shows on the taskbar, like it did in the Mac Dock. Click it to carry on here. A small iPhone or iPad badge shows where it's from.
- It appears and disappears the moment your device offers it. BugOS doesn't check over and over, so it costs nothing while idle.

**Make it yours** (BugOS › Taskbar › Handoff)
- Show it as its own little glass island in the taskbar's bottom-right corner (the default), next to your apps after a thin divider like the Mac Dock, or turn it off.
- Turn the device badge or the island's glass background on or off.

Needs Handoff on in System Settings › General › AirDrop & Handoff, on both the Mac and the other device.
