Fixes from the first round of feedback.

**Apps no longer drop off the taskbar or Alt-Tab**
- A full-screen app (Roblox, a game, a video) lives on its own desktop, so the taskbar lost track of it while you were elsewhere, and lost your other apps while you were in it. Full-screen windows now always count, and apps whose windows are on another desktop stay on the taskbar; clicking one takes you there.
- Alt-Tab shows them too ("On another desktop"), so minimized and full-screen windows are all there.

**Shift + scroll is now a choice** (BugOS › Misc)
- **Mac**: the Mac's own behaviour (a mouse wheel scrolls sideways with Shift).
- **Sideways**: always sideways with Shift, trackpad too, like Windows. (1.5.0's switch only worked for trackpads.)
- **Up and down**: Shift never turns scrolling sideways, for games where Shift is sprint and the wheel zooms, like Roblox.
