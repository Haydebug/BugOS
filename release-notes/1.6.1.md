The speed update. Same features, a lot less work.

**Faster**
- The taskbar uses about **90% less CPU and energy** when idle (measured against 1.6.0, both freshly started): better battery life, a cooler Mac. The music widget's progress bar was redrawing the whole taskbar five times a second, even while paused; now only the line moves, only while playing.
- Play, pause and skip show up on the taskbar instantly (Spotify and Music announce them).
- Window previews and peek open with their pictures already there, instead of filling them in a moment later.
- Scrolling never waits behind the taskbar, so Shift + scroll and the mouse wheel can't stutter while it's busy.
- The taskbar reads windows in one round trip per window instead of about ten, and shares one full-screen check between all taskbars.
- All apps is ready on the first open after login.

**File Explorer**
- Remembers your pane widths and which folders you had expanded on the left.
- New tabs open at your start location, and so do ⌘N and restored tabs whose folder is gone (instead of always Home).
