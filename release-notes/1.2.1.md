A small Squiggle fix.

- The drawing window (after a snip, or Draw on a Recording) now behaves like a normal window: it opens on top, and clicking somewhere else sends it behind instead of keeping it pinned over everything.
- While a Squiggle window is open, Squiggle shows on the taskbar and in ⌘-Tab, so you can bring it back; when they're all closed it goes back to running in the background.
