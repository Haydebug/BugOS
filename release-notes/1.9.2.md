Force quit for stuck apps and pop-ups, Windows-style click focus, and a calmer Squiggle.

**Force quit**
- Right-click an app on the taskbar › **Force quit** closes it straight away, with no "save changes?" asking.
- Stuck pop-up that isn't even on the taskbar? Point at it and press **⌃⌥⌘Q**: the app that owns it is closed.
- Right-click a window preview for **Close window**, **Close all windows**, **Quit** and **Force quit**.

**Any button brings a window forward** (BugOS › Mouse & keyboard, on by default)
- Like Windows: a right or middle click on a window in the back brings it to the front, and the click still lands. Right-drag camera moves (Roblox Studio) work on the first try.

**Squiggle**
- Only the newest squiggle stays open. Opening a new one closes the old one instead of piling them up.
- Clicking the snip thumbnail now always brings the new squiggle to the front.

**Taskbar**
- Icons no longer drop off the bar and come back when the Mac is very busy.
