A taskbar on every display.

**Multiple displays**
- Like Windows, every screen gets its own taskbar along the bottom, with window previews, All apps and full-screen hiding. The main screen's keeps the music, the quick corner and Alt-Tab.
- Bars appear and disappear as you plug displays in and out.
- **Show apps on**: every app on every taskbar (default), or each app only on the taskbar of the screen its window is on (pinned apps on the main one).
- Windows are kept above the taskbar on every screen that has one.
- Turn it off in BugOS › Taskbar › Multiple displays to keep a single taskbar.

**Alt-Tab**
- New **Order** setting: Recently used (default), the taskbar's order, or A–Z. The first Tab always picks the window after the one you're in.

**Fixes**
- Long choices no longer spill out of their row on the BugOS page.
