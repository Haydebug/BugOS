The big Windows update.

**Window snapping** (new page: Snapping)
- **⌃⌥ ← →** snaps to a half; again sends the window to the next screen. **⌃⌥ ↑** maximizes (or the top quarter of a half), **⌃⌥ ↓** restores (or the bottom quarter, or minimizes).
- **Drag** a window to the left or right edge for a half, a corner for a quarter, with a preview of where it'll land. Drag a snapped window away to get its old size back.
- **Snap layouts**: drag a window to the top of the screen, or rest the pointer on its green button, and pick a zone.
- **Snap assist**: after snapping one window, the rest of the screen offers your other windows.
- Works across displays of any size, and stops above the taskbar on every screen.

**Full Screen** (new page)
- The **green button maximizes** like Windows: it fills the screen above the taskbar and stays on this desktop. Click again to restore. **⌘-click** for the Mac's real full screen.
- Choose what double-clicking a title bar does, and whether the taskbar hides for full-screen apps.

**Clipboard history** (new page: Clipboard)
- **⌃⌥V** shows your recent copies (text, images, files), newest first. Pick one to paste it where you are. Pin the ones you want to keep; they survive restarts.
- Copies that password managers mark as secret are never kept. Nothing leaves your Mac.

**Mouse** (new page)
- Pointer acceleration (Windows' "Enhance pointer precision"), pointer and trackpad speed, natural scrolling.
- **Mouse wheel: 3 lines per notch, no acceleration** by default, like Windows (or the Mac's own scrolling). Trackpads aren't affected.
- Shift + scroll moved here from Misc.
- New installs start with pointer acceleration off; what was there before comes back on uninstall.

**Desktops**
- **Show apps from**: this desktop (like Windows) or all desktops (like the Mac Dock, so nothing changes when you switch).
- Switching desktops shows each desktop's apps right away instead of emptying the bar first.
- Clicking an app whose windows are on another desktop opens it here: a new copy, or a new window. Apps that only run once (Discord, Spotify, Slack…) take you to their window, and keep their underline on every desktop.
- Your taskbar order is kept when you switch desktops and back.

**Fixes**
- With several displays, a taskbar from an unplugged screen could come back on top of the main one, making every app look doubled.
- Snap assist redesigned; clipboard history's labels no longer overlap.

**Settings**
- The sidebar is grouped (Taskbar & windows, Shortcuts, Apps, Input) and has a search box that finds settings by what they do.
- The Permissions page says "You're good to go!" once everything's on.
