Alt+Enter for full screen, and hold-to-repeat works in every app.

**Alt+Enter toggles full screen** (BugOS › Full Screen, off by default)
- Like Windows: press ⌥↩ (Option is the Mac's Alt key) and the window you're in goes full screen. Press it again to come back. You can pick a different shortcut there too.

**Hold a key to repeat it, everywhere**
- Holding a letter now repeats it even in apps that were already open when you turned the setting on (before, they still showed the accent menu until you reopened them).
- Only while you're typing in a text field: in games, holding W to walk is still one long press.
