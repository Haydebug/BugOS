Alt-Tab, Windows 11 style.

- Hold **⌥ Option** and tap **Tab**: a panel in the middle of the screen shows a live thumbnail of every window, with its app icon and title, most recently used first.
- The first Tab already picks the window you were in before, so a quick ⌥Tab flips between your last two windows.
- Tab or → forward, Shift-Tab or ← back, ↑ ↓ a row at a time. Let go of ⌥ to switch. Esc cancels; Return or Space switches now; you can also point at a window and click it.
- Switches between windows, not just apps, including minimized windows and hidden apps (you can leave those out).
- New **Alt-Tab** page in BugOS: turn it on or off, choose ⌥ Tab or ⌘ Tab (⌘ Tab replaces the Mac's own app switcher), and pick small, medium or large previews.
- Opens on the screen your pointer is on.
