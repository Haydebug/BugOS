Hold a key and it repeats, like on Windows.

**Hold a key to repeat it** (BugOS › Mouse & keyboard › Keyboard, off by default)
- Hold E and it types eeeee instead of showing the accent menu. Hold Backspace and it keeps deleting, fast.
- Starts after a short pause (375ms), then repeats about 33 times a second, Windows' default speed. Takes effect straight away; an app that was already open may need reopening before holding a letter repeats in it.
- Uses the Mac's own key-repeat settings. Turning it off, or uninstalling BugOS, puts back exactly what you had.

**Also new**
- Taskbar: with "Show the taskbar on all displays" off, pick which screen the one taskbar goes on (BugOS › Taskbar › Show the taskbar on).
- Quick Corner: a Trigger area setting. Automatic uses a strip along the edge that's still a wall, so a screen below or beside this one (like an iPad) no longer makes the corner hard to hit.
- Window previews show every time you move along the taskbar, and close properly (they kept capturing windows in the background after closing).
