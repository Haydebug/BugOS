BugOS 1.4.0
===========

A Windows 11-style taskbar, File Explorer and screenshot tool for your Mac, in one app.
Needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) on macOS 15 Sequoia or newer.


INSTALL
1. Drag BugOS onto the Applications folder.
2. Open BugOS from Applications.
   The first time, macOS says it can't check BugOS for malicious software (it isn't
   from the App Store). Click Done, then open System Settings › Privacy & Security,
   scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to BugOS. Open it once more.
3. The BugOS page opens at Permissions. Click "Turn On…" next to each one and
   switch BugOS on in the page that opens. Click Done.

That's it. BugOS starts by itself when you log in, and folders open in File Explorer.


SETTINGS
Open BugOS any time (Applications or Spotlight) for its page:
  Features       turn the taskbar, File Explorer or Squiggle on and off
  Taskbar        size, icon alignment, background and opacity, previews, music
  Alt-Tab        hold Option (or Command) and tap Tab to switch windows, like Windows
  Quick Corner   the fan from the bottom-right corner: order, colours, curve, add apps
  Quick Access   swipe two fingers in from the trackpad's right edge (or your own key)
  File Explorer  folders, Save/Open dialogs, new-window shortcut, views, panes
  Squiggle       every shortcut (change or add your own), save folder, audio
  General        updates, accent colour, start at login, the Mac Dock, restart a part, uninstall


UNINSTALL
Open BugOS › General › Uninstall. It puts your Mac back how it was: your Dock and
hot corner, Finder for folders, no login item, no permission entries.
Your screenshots and recordings are never deleted.
